

# Fremtidens helsetjeneste vil bestå av nye tjenester hvor innbygger kan gjøre mer selv

![Diagram of future health services showing a flow from personal advice and service guides to automated services and integrated digital services, with a feedback loop for improvement and quality assurance.](images/Konsept%20digital%20f%C3%B8rstelinje/e84c7434834958f49141d04130194231_img.jpg)

The diagram illustrates the components of future health services, showing a flow from personal advice and service guides to automated services and integrated digital services, with a feedback loop for improvement and quality assurance.

On the left, five circular icons represent diverse individuals: an older man with glasses, a man with a red cap and sunglasses, a woman with dark hair, a woman with red hair, and a woman using a smartphone. Arrows from these icons point to the main service flow.

The main service flow is contained within a large light green box and consists of the following components and connections:

- Tjenesteveiviser** (Service guide) receives input from the individuals and points to **Personlige helseråd** (Personal health advice) and **Nasjonal nettlege** (National telehealth).
- Personlige helseråd** points to **Automatiserte helsetjenester** (Automated health services) and **Selvhjelpsverktøy** (Self-help tool).
- Nasjonal nettlege** points to **Selvhjelpsverktøy**.
- Selvhjelpsverktøy** points to **Integrerte digifysiske tjenester** (Integrated digital services).

Below the main service box, a feedback loop is shown:

- An arrow labeled **Forbedring** (Improvement) points from the bottom of the main box back to the **Tjenesteveiviser**.
- An arrow labeled **Utfall / opplevelse** (Outcome / experience) points from the bottom of the main box to a bar labeled **Sikre forsvarlighet og kvalitet** (Ensure accountability and quality).

The diagram illustrates the structure of a digital first line. On the left, five circular icons represent diverse users. A double-headed arrow connects these users to a central box labeled "Digital førstelinje". This box is also connected by a double-headed arrow to a large vertical stack of services. The services are grouped into three color-coded sections: green for new national digital services, blue for suppliers/producers, and pink for digital services acting as an entry point to primary and specialist health services. The green section includes: "Kvalitetssikret helseinformasjon", "Offentlig KI-tjeneste for målrettede helseråd", "Veiviser-funksjoner", "Automatiserte helsetjenester", and "Digital tvilling/helsearkiv". The blue section includes "Selvhjelsapper". The pink section includes "Nettlege", "Integrerte digi-fysiske tjenester", and "Automatiserte spesialisthelsetjenester". A large grey arrow points from the right side of the service stack to two boxes on the far right: "Primær-helsetjenesten" and "Spesialist-helsetjenesten". A legend on the right explains the color coding: a green square for "Nye nasjonale digitale tjenester", a blue square for "Leverandører/produsenter (godkjenningsordning)", and a pink square for "Digitale tjenester som inngang til primær- og spesialist-helsetjenester (premissgiver og godkjenningsrolle)".
