Source	Role	Scope
TB2026-17	The mandate (5 deloppdrag, frist 31.12.2026)	Broad: KI-tjeneste, selvhjelpsverktøy, psykisk helse/rus, forebygging, digital veiviser
Konsept-deck	The vision, 2 slides	Broad: 9 services incl. nettlege, digital tvilling

Structural problem #1: the deck and the målbilde are at different scopes but use overlapping words. Nowhere is it stated that Digital førstelinje is the umbrella and the økosystem-målbilde is one component inside it. Say this explicitly on an early slide — it's currently the single biggest source of confusion.

Main communication points for the vision
The strongest line in your entire material is buried in the målbilde and absent from the slides:

Digitalt når det går, menneskelig når det trengs.

Lead with it. Then six messages, in this order:

Én dør — one recognisable, quality-assured entry instead of today's fragmented market.
Samme forløp — egenmestring and helsehjelp are not two tracks; the tool the citizen starts alone carries over to the clinician, and back.
Tillit gjennom offentlig forankring — use the målbilde's best analogy: digital tools enter helsetjenestens verktøykasse på linje med legemidler. That single comparison conveys quality assurance, prescribing, follow-up and reimbursement at once.
Kapasitet dit den trengs mest — the sustainability argument that ties directly to the TB.
Vi måler at det virker — utfall/forbedring as documented effect, not assumption.
Forutsigbart marked — include only for supplier/industry audiences.
One framing risk worth fixing
Slide 1's title — "hvor innbygger kan gjøre mer selv" — reads as offloading work onto patients, and user organisations will hear cost-cutting. Reframe to what the citizen gets, e.g. "Innbyggeren får mer støtte, tidligere — og hjelp av folk når det trengs."

Making the slides more striking
Fix the terminology first. The same concept has two names across two slides, which quietly destroys comprehension:

Selvhjelpsverktøy vs Selvhjelsapper (also misspelled)
Integrerte digifysiske vs Integrerte digi-fysiske
Tjenesteveiviser vs Veiviser-funksjoner
Personlige helseråd vs Offentlig KI-tjeneste for målrettede helseråd
Pick one canonical label per concept and use it identically everywhere.

Replace 2 dense slides with a 7-slide arc:

Hvorfor nå — the problem, using the målbilde's from→to list. Pick 4, not all 9. Two columns, red left, green right. This is your most persuasive existing content and it isn't in the deck at all.
Løftet — the one-liner, full bleed, no diagram.
Kari, 34, sover dårlig — one citizen walking the same boxes as your flow diagram. The architecture becomes the backdrop of a story instead of the subject.
Hva vi bygger — the current slide 1 flow, built up progressively rather than shown at once.
Hvem har ansvar — the current slide 2. Keep the colour coding; it's genuinely good, but it's a governance answer and should never share a slide with the citizen value story.
Hvordan vi vet at det virker — promote Utfall/opplevelse and Forbedring. Today they're thin arrows at the bottom, visually subordinate, yet they are the trust engine.
Veivalg — what you need decided.
Two visual fixes:

Sikre forsvarlighet og kvalitet is drawn as a bar under the flow. It's a precondition, not a final step — make it the frame around the whole diagram.
Slide 2 asks the audience to absorb 9 services plus a 3-colour legend at once. Cluster them into four verbs: finn fram → få råd → gjør selv → få hjelp, then reveal the 9 underneath.
Mark what is not yet decided. The målbilde is explicit that it takes no position on the sentrale veivalg, but the deck presents Nasjonal nettlege, Digital tvilling/helsearkiv and Automatiserte spesialisthelsetjenester as settled architecture. Use a visual convention (dotted outline) for ambition-not-decision, or discussions will derail into "who decided this?".

Finally, the alt text in the converted deck reveals the diagrams carry a lot of meaning in labels alone — worth checking each diagram still reads correctly when projected, since several labels are long enough to become unreadable at the back of a room.
