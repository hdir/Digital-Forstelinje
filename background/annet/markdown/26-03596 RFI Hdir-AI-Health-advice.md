# Invitation to market dialogue through a prior information notice

## **Would you like to help shape a new national service for targeted health advice?**

The Norwegian Directorate of Health (Helsedirektoratet), hereinafter referred to as the “Contracting Authority”, and the Norwegian Health Network (Norsk helsenett SF) invite the market to participate in a dialogue on how artificial intelligence, quality-assured knowledge sources and digital health services that can be combined to provide citizens with safe and quality-assured health advice.

For the purposes of this market dialogue, targeted health advice means individually tailored health advice based on information about the individual, providing tailored responses directly to that individual and going beyond general health information.

We are seeking input from the market to explore potential solutions and implementation models.

At the time of publication of this notice, the Norwegian Directorate of Health is considering one or more procurements.

## **Description**

In order to strengthen the market's ability to participate in any subsequent procurement process and to facilitate effective procurements, the Contracting Authority invites suppliers to participate in market dialogue in accordance with Sections 12-1 and 12-2 of the Norwegian Public Procurement Regulations (FOA)

The Contracting Authority is initiating a dialogue with the market to obtain further information and insight relating to the needs described in this request.

If considered appropriate, the Contracting Authority may conduct one-to-one meetings with a selection of suppliers that submit written input following the planned information meeting

### **Description of the assignment**

The Norwegian Ministry of Health and Care Services (Helse- og omsorgsdepartementet) has requested the Norwegian Directorate of Health to establish a public AI service for health-related questions, in collaboration with Norwegian Health Network, the Norwegian Institute of Public Health (Folkehelseinstituttet) and the Norwegian Medical Products Agency (Direktoratet for medisinske produkter).

The service may initially be established as a first phase, with the ambition of further developing it into a more permanent service.

Through this market dialogue, the Contracting Authority seeks to establish a sound basis for assessing how the supplier market can contribute to the establishment, further development and ongoing management of the service.

More specifically, the Contracting Authority invites input on one or more of the following topics:

- solutions, services and concepts that can contribute to realising all or parts of a new national public AI service providing targeted health advice
- experience with comparable solutions in Norway and internationally
- approaches to management of knowledge, content and information sources
- approaches to evaluation and quality assurance of targeted health advice
- how the solution could interact and integrate with other services, knowledge sources and content on a national public platform such as Helsenorge
- status of, and experience with, CE marking of relevant solutions under the Medical Device Regulation (MDR)
- relevant collaboration and delivery models, including models involving multiple parties
- any other matters that you consider relevant

It should be emphasised that this market dialogue is being conducted for the purpose of gathering information and market insight. The descriptions in this material should not be understood as final requirements or decisions regarding the future solution or any subsequent procurement

The Contracting Authority asks suppliers to prepare to provide input on the topics described in this material and on topics that will subsequently be presented at an information meeting on 15 October 2026 from 13:00 to 15:00 CEST.

The information meeting will be conducted in English to facilitate participation by interested parties from Norway and other European countries. The meeting will be held physically, with the option to participate remotely. Further practical information will be provided to registered participants.

## **Timetable for the market dialogue**

- Deadline for registering interest: 12 October 2026
- Information meeting: 15 October 2026
- Deadline for submitting responses to specific questions: 22 October 2026
- The questions will be presented at the information meeting.
- One-to-one meetings with selected suppliers: 26–30 October 2026
